Introduction¶

AutoEDA is a Streamlit-based platform for automated exploratory data analysis and data preprocessing.

The goal of AutoEDA is to reduce the time required to understand and prepare datasets by providing statistical summaries, visualizations, feature stability analysis, and preprocessing tools.
